How it works

  1. Add a room and upload its 360° photo (2:1 “equirectangular” image from a 360° camera or a phone app such as Google Street View).
  2. On the room page, click on the photo to place an info dot, then write what it is.
  3. Add arrows on the floor so visitors can walk to the next room.
  4. Turn the view to the best angle and click “Use this view as the start”.
